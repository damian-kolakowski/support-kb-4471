The #4471 page says one issue, one fix queued. The reports say otherwise. Run the desk's own cause logic over everything merged into #4471 and three problems come out, each with its own ticket in the tracker repo:

  1. The shared parser reads dates month first. Tenants here write the day first, so a typed date lands in the wrong month. This is the bug #4471 knows about, and most reports belong to it. → tracker ticket #1
  2. The booking-v3 form's date hint is wrong. Under its date field it prints MM/DD/YYYY, people type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. Same symptom, different bug and different fix: every affected report comes from the three booking-v3 tenants, through the form. Engineering will not fix it, because the booking-v3 form is retired. → tracker ticket #2, closed as won't fix
  3. Some bookings move by one day, not one month. The desk footnoted these while merging: June-only shifts, a Lisbon and a Toronto reader seeing different days for one meeting, an invite dated before its booking. That is time-zone or DST handling, and no date parser fix will touch it. The engineering update on #4471 decided the other two causes and says nothing about this one, so it is still open. → tracker ticket #3, open
Not one issue. Three, merged under one subject, because decision 0001 merges on subject alone and every date bug gets the same subject. The counts below are computed from the export at render time.
